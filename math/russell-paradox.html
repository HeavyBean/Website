RUSSELL'S PARADOX

Russel's Paradox highlights a contradiction within naive set theory. Naive set theory was created by George Cantor in the late 1800s and was looked at with keen eyes with those that strived to root the foundations of mathematics in logic.

\[ let \quad R = \{x \mid x \notin x\}. \quad Then \quad R \in R \iff R \notin R \]
